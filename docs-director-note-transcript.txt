Welcome. You are the new Bharat Digital Fellow assigned to my department.
Sit. Have some tea. Let me tell you why you are here.
The old age pension is Rs.250 a month for any resident of Gurwanchal aged 60 or above.
For a lot of people, it is a lifeline. Seva Setu is its portal. A citizen verifies her
mobile with an OTP, fills in her details and bank account, uploads an age proof
and gets an application number. The block officer sees the queue and approves or rejects.
If nobody decides within 15 days, the law approves it automatically through the system.
My team has given you the code and data. Last month in Sonari block,
a woman of 70 came to the counter with her grandson. On the bus, he had asked an AI
assistant on his phone, Chad GPT or Claude, to apply for her pension. It could not.
It even gave outdated information on the scheme. It had no way to talk to our portal
and make an application in a way that works for AI agents. And here is the thing,
they were okay to use the AI agent but found our website not so easy.
I have heard the story with small variations from four blocks,
grandsons, daughters, CSC operators themselves. If these assistants can book flights and manage
calendars, why can they not help a widow apply for what the law already gives her?
Fellows from your last cohort built an MCP servers or AI assistants could use Ministry
of Statistics official digger. Do the same but not for reading data,
for applying for something and getting an answer. So this is the job. Make it possible for any AI
assistant to help a citizen apply for the pension and follow it up.
Built on open standards, the assistant talks to the portal through an official channel for agents,
the portal remains the system of record. My IT team says AI will hallucinate,
raise privacy concerns, skip validations. There must be a way to do this reasonably well.
Let's make the agent only work for a subset of blocks of the portal.
By properly I mean this. An AI assistant is just another way to apply,
following the same rules and validations as the portal or that such a form naturally should have.
The assistant must never get anyone a pension, the counter or paper form would not.
An application made from either place must be visible in both.
Our applicants are old, they share phones, they sit at kiosks,
the assistant must work for them, not for an engineer in a demo.
Some of the worry inside my office may ease if the approving officers get an AI assistant too.
How can it make their life easier and help them do the job well?
She should see what is waiting for her, look at a case and approve or reject it for the
reason recorded against her name. That is a different design.
I will test it by pointing my own AI assistant at what you build
